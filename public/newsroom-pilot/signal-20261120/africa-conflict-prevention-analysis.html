Analysis: Africa’s conflict-prevention gap is action after warning.

In an SABC News opinion essay, Prof. Adeoye O. Akinola argues that early-warning systems matter only when institutions have the political commitment, financing and coordination to act.

What the author argues

Akinola argues that early-warning systems cannot prevent conflict on their own. Institutions must translate information into decisions, support local peacebuilding knowledge and sustain prevention financially.

Why It Matters

The analysis shifts attention from crisis response to governance, community knowledge, African agency, institutional coherence and peace financing.

What We’re Watching

  • Any published recommendations from the African Union Studies symposium referenced by the essay.
  • New funding or institutional commitments.
  • Evidence that early-warning information is converted into preventive action.
