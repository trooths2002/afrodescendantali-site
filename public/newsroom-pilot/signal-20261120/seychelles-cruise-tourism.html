Seychelles pushes cruise tourism toward higher value, lower impact.

Seychelles is steering the 2026–2027 cruise season toward higher-value, lower-impact visits and stronger local-business participation while a dedicated strategy continues to develop.

What We Know

Seychelles News Agency reported that officials are emphasizing higher-value, lower-impact cruise visits and measures intended to help local businesses benefit during the 2026–2027 season.

Why It Matters

The policy question is how much cruise value remains with Seychellois businesses while ecological and community pressures are managed.

What We’re Watching

  • Final strategy language and implementation dates.
  • Passenger-spend and local-business measures.
