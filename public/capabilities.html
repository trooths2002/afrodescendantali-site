Evidence-backed capability families

Start from the problem, not the jargon.

A project may combine several of these capabilities. The purpose of the catalog is to make the underlying client job recognizable without forcing you to diagnose which tool or internal workflow is needed.

Research & Verification

Make claims defensible before they become public.

Source hierarchy, claim verification, contradiction search, provenance, uncertainty classification, and safe wording for research, media, and decisions.

Legal & Public-Affairs Media Support

Build accurate media under deadline pressure.

Rapid intake, event-fact reconciliation, attributed allegation language, rights-sensitive imagery checks, production briefs, clips, graphics, and approval queues.

Archive, Story & Documentary Systems

Turn scattered archives into chronology, story, and knowledge.

Archive inventory, transcript mining, evidence chronology, narrative–asset mapping, documentary paper edits, and multi-format resource extraction.

Digital Business Launch Systems

Claim-safe messaging, approved asset recovery, landing-page previews, domain and mailbox readiness, phone-routing design, and release gates.

Institutional Knowledge & Memory Systems

Make organizational memory retrievable and operational.

Canonical-record audits, decision and action registers, folder and knowledge architecture, discrepancy reconciliation, dashboards, and institutional-memory workflows.

Content & Media Systems

Transcript-to-edit workflows, articles, short-form plans, graphics, landing pages, metadata, derivative assets, and publication-ready packages.

Structured Client Delivery
