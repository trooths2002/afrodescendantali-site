Mauritius hosts Indian Ocean regional health forum

The 22nd colloquium on HIV, hepatitis and addictions brought regional health stakeholders together in Quatre-Bornes from 18 to 20 November.

What We Know

Mauritius hosted the 22nd Indian Ocean colloquium on HIV, hepatitis and addictions from 18 to 20 November in Quatre-Bornes. Government information describes the theme as “bridging the gap” and frames the meeting as regional cooperation.

Who was represented

The communique names health stakeholders and delegates from Comoros, Madagascar, Mayotte, Rodrigues, Réunion and Seychelles alongside Mauritius.

Why It Matters

A regional forum can create a place to compare public-health practice and service gaps across islands. The meeting itself is evidence of coordination, not proof that health outcomes or services have changed.

What We’re Watching

The forum’s recommendations, follow-through by participating health systems, cross-border service coordination and any measurable changes in HIV, hepatitis or addiction support.
