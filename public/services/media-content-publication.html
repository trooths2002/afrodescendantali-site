Common questions

Can you work with footage or source material I already have?

Yes. Existing footage, photographs, transcripts, archives, or other source material can be the starting point when it is usable and you have the right to use it.

Do media packages guarantee views or subscriber growth?

No. I deliver the agreed production work; platform reach, virality, subscriber growth, and third-party distribution results are not guaranteed.

What affects documentary or archive scope?

Source volume, chronology, rights, transcription needs, story complexity, approvals, and editing requirements.
