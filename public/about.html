About

News and context for the Black world.

Afrodescendant Ali follows the people, institutions, and movements shaping Black life across Africa, the Caribbean, Black America, Afro-Latin America, Afro-Europe, and the Indian Ocean.

Our point of view

We connect stories across borders without pretending every community is the same. Kenya is an operating base and reporting vantage point; the audience and field are global.

Who we serve

Our readers want more than a headline: people building, organizing, researching, investing, creating, and deciding across the Black world.

How we report

Useful news before the noise.

Check the record

We separate documents, claims, allegations, official statements, and unknowns before drawing conclusions.

Follow who decides

We ask which court, agency, government, funder, platform, archive, or media system controls the outcome.

Explain why it matters

We explain why an issue matters across African, Afrodescendant, and diaspora communities without flattening the differences.

When you need more than the article

Readers can move from public coverage to a focused briefing, dossier, memo, audit, or follow-up research when the question becomes decision-specific.

What comes next

Trusted coverage, direct reader relationships, useful paid work.

The goal is simple: earn attention with useful journalism, build a direct relationship with readers, and offer deeper research when it helps people act.

Explore ways to work with us

Research systems, trade data infrastructure, and decision architecture operated in alignment with Ascend Global Ventures.
