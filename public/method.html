How I Work

Project process
01

Understand the goal

We start with the problem, desired result, audience, timing, and material you already have.

02

Review the source material

I check the files, facts, website state, records, or other inputs that will shape the work.

03

Confirm scope and price

You receive a written proposal defining deliverables, timing, investment, exclusions, and what I need from you.

04

Build the deliverable

I produce the agreed media, website, brief, communications package, system, or prototype.

05

Review and test

We check the work against the agreed requirements and complete the included review rounds.

06

Deliver and hand off

You receive the final agreed deliverables and any handoff notes, files, or operating guidance included in scope.
