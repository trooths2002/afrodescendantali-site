04 · Research & Intelligence

AfCFTA is easy to describe in headlines and much harder to evaluate in implementation. The project needed a way to keep sources, findings, unresolved questions, trade friction, and public explanation connected.

What was needed
Separate continental trade-policy promise from implementation evidence and make the research understandable to a public audience.

A public implementation watch with source guidance, findings, open questions, trade-friction views, explainers, and a short-form education layer.
What they received
A reusable research system that can grow as evidence changes rather than becoming a one-time article that goes stale.

06 · Business & Knowledge Systems

Scattered information became an operating system people could use.

When important history, commitments, files, decisions, and current actions live in different places, the problem is not simply storage. The project needs a structure that shows what matters now and preserves what should remain understandable later.

What was needed
Make years of material and current operating information easier to find, reconcile, review, and act on.
What I built
Source inventories, reconciled records, decision/action/risk registers, dashboard views, and repeatable memory/documentation workflows.
What they received
A clearer operating structure for current work and institutional memory instead of another folder full of disconnected documents.
