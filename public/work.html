Public-safe proof

Different problems. A consistent operating discipline.

The examples below focus on demonstrated work and deliverable state. They do not imply testimonials, legal representation, regulated-industry expertise, or client outcomes that have not been independently evidenced.

Research & public-interest media

Verify first. Publish second.

A recurring public-interest media need required faster verification of speech and incident claims before publication.

What was built
Transcript review, claim verification, authoritative-source research, safe wording, and evidence-backed paper edits.
Deliverable state
Evidence memo, verification queue, source trail, and short-form production package.
Boundary
Unsupported numbers stayed unverified instead of being repeated for impact.
Rapid-response public-affairs media

Move quickly without making uncertainty disappear.

Fast-moving public-affairs matters arrived through changing messages, event details, allegations, and sensitive image requests.

What was built
Separated workstreams, contradiction checks, attribution controls, image provenance checks, production briefs, and approval queues.
Deliverable state
A repeatable rapid-response media workflow for flyers, clips, campaign assets, and updates.
Boundary
Allegations remain attributed; this is media support, not legal representation.
Archive → story → knowledge

Turn years of material into a usable story system.

A multi-year first-person video archive existed without a reliable chronology, narrative architecture, or reusable knowledge layer.

What was built
Archive index, transcript mining, evidence chronology, narrative–asset matrix, documentary structure, and transferable lesson extraction.
Deliverable state
A system supporting documentary episodes, articles, checklists, and short-form derivatives.
Boundary
The evidence layer controls what the story can truthfully claim.
Service-business launch readiness

A credible launch is more than a redesigned page.

A professional service business had marketing goals, legacy web assets, claims, email, and phone routing that were not yet aligned.

What was built
Claim verification, approved-asset recovery, claim-safe previews, and domain, mailbox, and phone-routing launch gates.
Deliverable state
Marketing previews plus a launch-readiness control system.
Boundary
Professional credentials and infrastructure are verified before being represented as live.
Institutional website transformation

Make the website support the institution—not just describe it.

A member-based institution needed its website to support membership, engagement, publishing, identity, rights, and safe production changes.

What was built
Decision and risk controls, membership-first UX logic, identity/editorial standards, rights rules, live-state QA, and staged repository release controls.
Deliverable state
A governed institutional website transformation program.
Boundary
Implementation claims are separated from planned or unverified state.
Institutional knowledge & memory

Turn institutional memory into an operating system.

A long-running membership institution had scattered records, open commitments, governance conflicts, and valuable history that were difficult to navigate.

What was built
Canonical-root audit, non-destructive folder governance, decision/action/risk registers, dashboard architecture, reconciliation controls, and institutional-memory workflow.
Deliverable state
An operating-system and knowledge-memory architecture designed for governed adoption.
Boundary
AI and dashboards surface institutional state; they do not invent institutional authority.
What repeats

The projects are different. The operating discipline repeats.

Across research, media, archives, websites, and institutions, the same core work keeps appearing: establish source truth, structure the problem, verify what can be claimed, build the deliverable, test it, preserve approvals and evidence, then turn what was learned into a stronger reusable capability.
