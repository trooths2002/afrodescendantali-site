# Public-evidence market validation matrix

## Validation standard

Public evidence can validate that real organizations fund, commission, or publicly request work of these kinds. It cannot validate Ali-specific conversion, price acceptance, delivery time, or client satisfaction. Those remain unvalidated until real buyer responses or completed engagements exist.

## Evidence matrix

| Lane | Organization or program | Public buying trigger | Requested or funded deliverables | Match to Ali | Confidence | Limitation / overclaim risk |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Public narrative | African Development Bank / PIDA | A multinational infrastructure program needs a coherent public communication approach. | Communication strategy, stakeholder engagement, digital content, media relations, and monitoring. | Turn complex programs into clear public communication and usable content. | High | This validates institutional demand, not Ali’s eligibility for AfDB procurement. |
| Public narrative | UNESCO documentary-heritage work in Ghana and Mali | Documentary heritage requires preservation, access, capacity, and public awareness. | A funded 2025–2027 project covering preservation, access, capacity building, and awareness. | Make archive and heritage work legible to publics and partners. | High | Project funding does not prove demand for every proposed format. |
| Public narrative | UNESCO Memory of the World in Africa | African documentary heritage is underrepresented and needs stronger public awareness and policy support. | Expert training, preservation practice, public awareness, and policy strengthening. | Support public-facing heritage narratives and evidence-led interpretation. | High | UNESCO’s program is broader than a communications engagement. |
| Research and verification | Afreximbank Trade Intelligence Solutions | Fragmented trade, market, and customs information increases the cost of decisions in Africa. | Country and sector briefs, policy updates, market-entry, risk, and competitor analysis. | Research, verification, context, and decision-ready communication. | High | Afreximbank’s product and internal capability set are not proof of Ali’s pricing. |
| Research and verification | Afreximbank African Research and Innovation Hub | Research needs a bridge from academic work to policy, industry, and practical application. | Research, prototypes, practical application, and commercialization pathways. | Produce grounded research that can be understood and used by decision-makers. | Medium-high | The hub is an ecosystem program, not a direct service brief. |
| Public presence | IFC / World Bank digital opportunities in African businesses | African firms have a large unfinished digital-upgrade opportunity. | Digital tools, online presence, and business-process improvement are identified as opportunity areas. | Clarify what an organization offers, why it matters, and how people engage. | Medium | The report establishes opportunity, not a specific website purchase or buyer intent. |
| Shared institutional communication | African Development Bank communications roles and notices | Programs repeatedly need communication diagnosis, audience planning, messages, channels, and monitoring. | Communication strategies, stakeholder plans, messages, channels, and evaluation. | Combine research, narrative, and public-presence work around a defined problem. | High | Public notices show category demand; they do not establish Ali-specific competitive position. |

## What the evidence supports

The market signal is strongest where a Black World institution or project must make complex work understandable, defensible, and usable: institutional communication, Africa-focused research and verification, documentary heritage and public access, and specialist digital clarity.

The commercial message supported by this evidence is:

> **Clear public work for the Black World.**

Three buyer routes are legible:

1. Explain an important program, story, or project.
2. Get an answer you can stand behind.
3. Help people understand and engage with your organization.

## Claims this does not validate

- Ali-specific win rate or conversion rate.
- Price acceptance or minimum viable pricing.
- Delivery hours, capacity, or margin.
- Client satisfaction or repeat purchase.
- Permission to name or publish client work.
