## Attribution pass

Use language such as:

- According to [source], ...
- [Institution] announced ...
- Court records show ...
- The report found ...
- Residents described ...
- The available record does not establish ...
- This is an inference from ...
- Afrodescendant Ali could not independently verify ...

Do not use a source's allegation as a fact. Do not use “proves,” “shocking,” “explosive,” “historic” or “everyone is talking about” without precise support.
