# RSS-to-Original-Journalism Workflow v0.1

Date: 2026-10-28
Project: PM-009 — AfrodescendantAli.com
Status: bounded editorial workflow; automatic writing and publishing disabled

## Editorial principle

RSS provides discovery. Afrodescendant Ali creates the journalism.

A feed item is a lead, not a finished article. The editor opens the original source, verifies the claims, adds independent context, and writes an original piece.

## Workflow

1. DISCOVERY_ONLY — receive title, source, timestamp and direct URL.
2. SOURCE_OPENED — editor opens the original source and records the access time.
3. CLAIMS_IN_REVIEW — split the proposed article into checkable claims.
4. DRAFTING — write an original headline, dek, body and analysis.
5. EDITORIAL_REVIEW — check sourcing, rights, attribution, language, fairness and mobile presentation.
6. APPROVED_FOR_PUBLISH — owner/editor approval is recorded.
7. PUBLISHED — publish only through the approved production path.

Any unresolved rights, source, identity, conflict, safety or factual issue routes the item to HOLD.

## What Afrodescendant Ali may create

- Original news briefs
- Original analysis
- Context explainers
- Timelines
- Regional roundups
- Interviews
- Case files
- Editorial commentary
- Community reports

## Required source treatment

Every article must retain:

- original source name;
- original source URL;
- direct article URL where available;
- publication date/time;
- access date;
- source type;
- attribution language;
- rights state;
- claim ledger;
- editor and review status.

Preferred attribution:

> According to [source], [verified claim]. Afrodescendant Ali's reporting/context adds [independently supported information].

Do not imply that Afrodescendant Ali conducted reporting that it did not conduct.

## Prohibited automatic behavior

The workflow must not:

- turn an RSS item directly into a published article;
- copy article bodies, excerpts, images, video or audio;
- translate or lightly rewrite distinctive source phrasing without permission;
- present a source allegation as established fact;
- remove the original source link;
- publish an item with unresolved rights or missing direct attribution;
- send notifications or index a draft automatically.

## Claim review

Each material claim receives:

- claim ID;
- proposed wording;
- source reference;
- classification;
- confidence;
- risk level;
- approved wording;
- unresolved question, if any.

Use primary records where available. Attribute allegations to the person, filing, institution or report making them. Distinguish reporting, official statements, analysis and community testimony.

## Rights states

- link_only_allowed — source terms clearly permit the planned limited use;
- permission_required — written permission or partnership is needed;
- discovery_only — source is a lead only until terms are verified;
- excluded — do not use for public output.

Rights status is separate from factual verification. A fact can be verified while the source material remains unavailable for reuse.

## Article types

- NEWS_BRIEF — concise, source-grounded update;
- ANALYSIS — original interpretation with clearly identified evidence;
- EXPLAINER — context and background;
- TIMELINE — dated events with source links;
- CASE_FILE — structured record requiring heightened verification;
- COMMUNITY_REPORT — contributor-supplied material with consent and verification record;
- EDITORIAL — opinion clearly separated from reporting.

## Release criteria

An article is publishable only when:

- at least one reliable source supports every material claim;
- sensitive claims have stronger corroboration or careful attribution;
- rights state permits the planned use;
- the body is original Afrodescendant Ali writing;
- direct source links and attribution are present;
- images/media have separate rights clearance;
- an editor approves the item;
- mobile and failure-state presentation passes QA.

The current pilot remains metadata-only, editorially gated and non-production. This workflow does not authorize source activation, recurrence, automatic publication, external outreach or indexability.
