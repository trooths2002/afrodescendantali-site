# Commercial reset: offer and economics map

Status: internal working document for owner review. Public changes remain candidate-only and `noindex,nofollow`.

## 1. What is being sold

Afrodescendant Ali helps people and organizations make important work clear, credible, and usable in public.

The buyer is not purchasing a list of production tasks. They are purchasing a clearer public result: a story people can understand, an answer they can trust, or a presence that helps the right people act.

Ali's commercial sequence is:

`SIGNAL → UNDERSTANDING → TRUST → PUBLIC EXPRESSION`

When the need becomes organizational decision-making, revenue, systems, automation, or measurement, the work belongs with Ascend Global Ventures (AGV).

## 3. Primary buyer hypotheses

These are working hypotheses, not validated market facts. Validate them through conversations before treating them as a finance-ready market case.

1. **Founder-led and specialist service organizations** that are good at what they do but are hard to understand, trust, or contact online.
2. **Public-interest, cultural, educational, and media projects** carrying important stories, archives, or community knowledge that need a credible public form.
3. **Organizations and researchers facing a difficult public question** that requires source review, evidence reconstruction, and contextual explanation.

The homepage should serve these groups by problem, not by trying to address everyone.

## 4. One entry engagement per Ali lane

### A. Public Narrative / Documentary Engagement

**Best when:** an important story, archive, project, or public question needs to be documented and made understandable.

**Buyer receives:** a defined story angle; research and source plan; agreed narrative structure; the appropriate media, editorial, or publication result; and a handoff package for use or publication.

**Buyer provides:** access to people, places, archives, source material, permissions, decision-makers, and timely feedback.

**Next step:** a short scoping conversation to identify the audience, material, public outcome, format, timing, and confidentiality boundary.

### B. Research / Verification Engagement

**Best when:** a claim, history, institution, or public-affairs question cannot be responsibly answered by surface reading.

**Buyer receives:** a research question and source plan; an evidence trail; a briefing, memo, dossier, or public explanation matched to the need; and a clear distinction between established, uncertain, and unresolved findings.

**Buyer provides:** the question, known context, relevant documents or leads, intended audience, decision or publication context, and any access constraints.

**Next step:** a scoping conversation to establish the question, source boundary, level of rigor, delivery format, and review process.

### C. Public Presence Clarity Engagement

**Best when:** people need to understand who the buyer is, why the work matters, trust the proof, and know what to do next.

**Buyer receives:** clarified positioning; audience and message architecture; a public-facing page or site structure; proof presentation; inquiry pathway; and practical guidance for keeping the presence clear.

**Buyer provides:** current materials, offer or project details, audience knowledge, proof, access to decision-makers, approvals, and a realistic launch window.

**Next step:** a scoping conversation focused on the buyer's current confusion, priority audience, desired action, proof available, and implementation boundary.

## 5. Delivery and minimum viable pricing

No public price is set by this document. The internal minimum must be calculated from actual delivery conditions.

**Known:** the three lanes, the ownership boundary, the expected buyer inputs, and the delivery formats above.

**Inferred:** a paid scoping step is commercially useful because the work is assembled around a problem and cannot be responsibly priced from a generic task list.

**Missing:** delivery-hour baselines, loaded hourly cost, direct costs, revision burden, capacity, target gross margin, close rate, and price acceptance.

Use this internal formula once those inputs are supplied:

`minimum viable price = (delivery hours × loaded hourly cost + direct costs + contingency) ÷ (1 − target gross margin)`

Track each engagement separately:

`contribution = price − direct delivery cost`

`required qualified inquiries = target engagements ÷ close rate`

Until the missing inputs are measured, public language should invite scope rather than promise a starting price. The first validation work is three to five buyer conversations per lane, followed by time tracking on the first engagements.

## 6. Proof architecture

| Proof type | Public treatment | Current boundary |
|---|---|---|
| Client proof | Named only with explicit permission; otherwise generalized and anonymized. | No named-client proof published. |
| Anonymous proof | Describe the buyer problem, intervention, deliverable, and observable change without identifying clues. | Requires confidentiality check and owner approval per case. |
| Demonstration work | Clearly label as demonstration or generalized example. | Must not imply a client relationship. |
| Owned work: AfCFTA Implementation Watch | Present as Ali's independent research desk and proof of method. | It is not a client case study or a service product. |

An anonymous case study is credible when it names the problem, scope, method, evidence boundary, deliverable, and observable outcome while removing names, exact dates, locations, uncommon figures, and identifying combinations. Credibility comes from inspectable method and boundaries, not from disguising a client with vague language.

## 7. Public messaging decisions

Homepage promise: **Make complex work clear, credible, and usable.**

Three problem routes:

- Need to explain or publish something important? Start with Public Narrative / Documentary.
- Need to know what is true or what the evidence says? Start with Research / Verification.
- Need people to understand, trust, find, and contact you? Start with Public Presence Clarity.
