## Publication rule

No case study moves to the public site until the owner confirms the wording and the relevant client or confidentiality record permits anonymous use. “Anonymous” does not mean “permission-free.” Any detail that could identify a client through combination must be removed, generalized, or explicitly cleared.

## Classification scale

- **Evidence strength — High:** a public or inspectable artifact exists and the work performed can be described from that artifact without relying on an unsupported claim.
- **Evidence strength — Medium:** a visible artifact or public output exists, but scope, client confirmation, or outcome evidence is incomplete.
- **Evidence strength — Low:** the work is described in the repository but lacks a sufficiently inspectable artifact or verified outcome.
- **Re-identification risk — High:** the combination of sector, geography, subject, artifact, dates, or distinctive details could identify the client.
- **Re-identification risk — Medium:** identification is possible to an informed local or sector audience but not obvious to a general visitor.
- **Re-identification risk — Low:** the description can be generalized without a meaningful identification path.
