## How to use the samples

Use the samples to evaluate:

- clarity of the lede;
- specificity of geography and affected people;
- sentence rhythm;
- distinction between reporting and analysis;
- attribution and uncertainty;
- regional connection without flattening differences;
- strength and restraint of the “what to watch” ending.

Samples are references, not published copy. They do not authorize source reuse, automatic article generation, publication or indexability.

Every new sample should include its source record, claim notes, rights state and editorial status.
